# HRG 黑白抽象 Logo

使用内置 imagegen 生成，采用斜切块面、错位关系和贯穿中心的负空间。

## 提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original abstract graphic logo for HRG, a competitive rhythm-game outdoor team event.
Create ONE finished standalone symbol on a square WHITE canvas, BLACK graphic only. Strict two-color design: solid #000000 and solid #FFFFFF. No grayscale shading, no gradients, no colored accents, no texture, no lighting, no 3D, no mockup, no words or letters.

Visual idea: a compact, original fractured square emblem assembled from sharply cut, interlocking black geometric planes. The planes lean and pull in different directions yet belong to one strong silhouette, evoking multiple teams moving through a game and ultimately converging. Use controlled asymmetry, powerful diagonals, offset masses, acute cuts, and a continuous white negative-space route through the center. The image should have palpable visual tension, like a restrained burst frozen at the instant of acceleration. Not random scattered shards: one coherent tightly composed mark with precision-cut joins and bold negative space.

Style: sophisticated geometric graphic design, esports energy without a stock esports shield, the angular and cut-corner language of HRG's interface distilled into a symbol. Design remains unmistakable in a small app icon and on a large poster. No identifiable city landmarks, music-note cliché, checkerboard filler, target symbol, mascot, shield, five-point star, pinwheel, generic lightning bolt, or literal H/R/G letterform. Aim for a distinctive original silhouette, graphic rhythm, intentional construction.
Composition: logo occupies roughly 65–70% of the square canvas, centered optically with generous white safety margins, no border. Crisp flat vector-like black fills and exact white negative space. A finished identity mark, not a concept sheet or multiple options. High-resolution square output.
